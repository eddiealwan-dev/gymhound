# GymHound

## Product strategy (owner, 2026-10-13)

Use this framing for App Store copy, screenshots, and every future feature decision.

**Core differentiator: "The anti-algorithm strength coach."** Progressive overload that is transparent and editable.
Every exercise shows its exact rule (e.g. "Hit 8s on a normal day and next time is 140 lb"), why it progressed or
held, and lets the lifter override the weight or change the rule. It pairs with the **Something off** flags (a
marked day holds the weight). This is the main reason someone would leave Strong or Hevy, and the thing they will
screenshot and share.

Supporting angles:
- **Data portability:** easy CSV import and export, plus a clean, shareable strength report (like a yearly summary).
- **Quiet, minimalist logger:** no feed, no guilt, fast logging that gets out of the way.

When weighing a feature, ask whether it strengthens the transparent progression rule first, then portability or
quiet logging. Lead App Store copy and the first screenshots with the visible rule and its "why".
